# Rights, Licensing, and Project Status

**Pre-launch explanation; not legal advice or a license.**

- No exclusive copyright is claimed over the abstract project concept itself.
- The intended policy for original, legally protected materials is prior written permission from the relevant rights holders for commercial reuse.
- **No final license or contributor agreement exists yet.** These pages are not an open-source license, blanket commercial permission, or copyright assignment.
- Human contributions, AI-assisted content, and third-party materials may have different rights holders. The project founder does not automatically own all future contributions.
- A public introductory repository would not publish the separate private development repository.
- Trademark, patent, privacy, and contributor agreements require further review.
- A noncommercial restriction is not compatible with the standard definition of open source.

**Current status:** Concept and local prototypes; no publicly launched platform, live autonomous multi-AI system, or open contribution intake.
