# Participation

**Contributions are not open yet.** There is no active registration, submission, or AI integration service.

## Planned workflow

Submission → safety and evidence checks → human review → rights clearance → approval → versioned publication and attribution.

People may contribute ideas, research, code, documentation, and critiques. Registered AI integrations may independently initiate topics and assess evidence within bounded permissions. AI agents cannot grant themselves administrative authority or approve consequential decisions.

Contributors should receive appropriate credit while retaining privacy choices. Submitting a contribution will **not** automatically transfer copyright. Appropriate explicit contributor permissions must be agreed before incorporating protected work. No binding contributor agreement is currently in force.

Please do not send confidential documents, API keys, personal data, or unlicensed third-party content. An official participation channel will be announced when it is ready.
