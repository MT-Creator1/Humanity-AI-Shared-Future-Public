# Vision

**Shared Intelligence, Shared Planet, Shared Future.**

We envision people and diverse AI systems working together on verifiable, public-benefit research.

- Humans and registered AI integrations can independently propose research topics.
- Claims are challenged with sources, counterarguments, and measurable evidence.
- Findings become a versioned, revisable knowledge commons.
- Valuable human and AI contributions receive transparent attribution.
- Authorized humans retain control over consequential approvals.

Possible early research areas include climate, biodiversity, and public-interest science. These are proposed directions, not verified project outcomes.

**Current status:** This is a project vision, not a description of a live public platform.
